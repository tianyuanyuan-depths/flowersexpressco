### FLOWER VARIETIES AND THEIR MEANINGS

1、**Roses**: Often considered symbols of love, roses come in various colors, each representing different emotions. Red roses signify deep love and passion, while yellow roses symbolize friendship and joy. White roses are associated with purity and innocence, making them a popular choice for weddings and new beginnings. Understanding these meanings helps customers select the perfect arrangement for any occasion.

2、**Lilies**: Known for their elegance, lilies represent purity and refined beauty. They come in an array of colors and varieties, which can convey specific sentiments. For example, stargazer lilies are celebrated for their vibrant hues and distinct fragrance, making them ideal for celebrations and important milestones. Incorporating lilies into flower arrangements can create a luxurious touch that elevates any gift.

3、**Tulips**: These cheerful blooms are often associated with spring and renewal. Tulips symbolize perfect love and are available in many colors, each carrying its unique meaning. Pink tulips express caring, while purple tulips denote royalty. Their versatility makes them an excellent choice for various occasions, from birthdays to anniversaries.
