## MEANINGS BEHIND VARIOUS FLOWER TYPES

Flowers carry significant meanings and symbolism, which can enhance the sentiment behind any gift. Here are a few popular varieties and their representations:

1. **Roses** - Often associated with love and passion, different colors of roses convey unique messages. For example, red roses symbolize deep love, while yellow roses signify friendship.
   
2. **Lilies** - These elegant flowers stand for purity and refined beauty. They are a popular choice for both celebratory and memorial occasions.

3. **Tulips** - Representing perfect love, tulips are ideal for romantic gestures and spring celebrations. Their vibrant colors bring joy and warmth to any arrangement.

4. **Orchids** - The exotic beauty of orchids symbolizes luxury and strength. Their uniqueness makes them a favored choice for special occasions.

Understanding the meanings behind different flowers can help you choose the right bouquet for your message, ensuring that your gift resonates with the recipient.
