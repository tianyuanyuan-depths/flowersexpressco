## MEANINGS BEHIND VARIOUS FLOWER VARIETIES

Flowers are not just beautiful; they carry deep meanings and emotions. For instance, roses symbolize love, while lilies represent purity. Understanding these meanings can elevate the impact of your gift. Here’s a glimpse into some popular flowers and their significance:

1. **Roses**: Often given to express love and passion, different colors convey various emotions—red for love, white for innocence, and yellow for friendship.
   
2. **Lilies**: Known for their elegance, lilies signify purity and refined beauty, making them a perfect choice for celebrations or formal events.

3. **Gerberas**: Cheerful and vibrant, gerberas symbolize joy and are ideal for uplifting someone's spirits.

4. **Tulips**: These flowers represent grace and perfection. They are often gifted to express care and affection.

By selecting flowers that resonate with the recipient's personality, each bouquet transforms into a personalized gift imbued with a deeper meaning.
