## THE SIGNIFICANCE OF FLOWER VARIETIES

Different flowers hold varied meanings, contributing to their significance in gifting. Roses, for instance, symbolize love and romance, while lilies are often associated with purity and renewal. **Daisies represent innocence and joy**, making them ideal for cheerful arrangements. Understanding these meanings can profoundly impact the choice of flowers for different events, enhancing the emotional connection conveyed through your gift.

For celebrations such as birthdays, anniversaries, or graduations, a **mixed bouquet featuring seasonal flowers** can convey warmth and affection. Alternatively, for somber occasions, subtle arrangements of white chrysanthemums or orchids may express sympathy and support. **Flowers are not merely gifts; they carry messages that resonate deeply with the recipient**, and selecting the right variety can add a personal touch to your thoughtful gesture.
