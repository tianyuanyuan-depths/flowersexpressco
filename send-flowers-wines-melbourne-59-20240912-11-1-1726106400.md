## VARIETIES OF FLOWERS AND THEIR MEANING

Flowers carry different meanings, adding layers to the act of giving. Understanding the significance of various blooms can enhance the sentiment behind your gift. Here are some popular flower varieties you might consider:

1. **Roses:** Often seen as symbols of love and romance, red roses convey deep emotions, while yellow roses represent friendship and joy. Pink roses symbolize gratitude and admiration.
   
2. **Lilies:** These elegant flowers represent purity and refined beauty. White lilies are often associated with chastity, while orange lilies can symbolize passion and confidence.

3. **Tulips:** A classic choice for spring, tulips express elegance and grace. They come in various colors, each carrying its meaning; for instance, purple tulips signify royalty and nobility, while red tulips are declarations of love.

4. **Daisies:** Simple yet charming, daisies symbolize innocence and purity. They can brighten anyone's day with their cheerful appearance, making them perfect for casual gifts.

5. **Chrysanthemums:** In many cultures, these flowers symbolize optimism and joy. They are versatile and come in multiple shapes and colors, catering to diverse preferences.

By selecting flowers that resonate with the recipient's personality and your relationship, you can communicate thoughtful messages beyond words.
