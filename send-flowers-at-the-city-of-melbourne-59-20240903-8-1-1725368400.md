### SIGNIFICANCE OF VARIOUS FLOWER TYPES

When selecting flowers, understanding their meanings can add an extra layer of thoughtfulness to your gift. Each flower species carries its own symbolism:

1. **Roses**: Often associated with love and romance, red roses symbolize deep passion, while yellow roses represent friendship and joy.
2. **Lilies**: These elegant blooms convey purity and refined beauty, making them suitable for various occasions, including weddings or as sympathy flowers.
3. **Carnations**: Known for their frilliness, these flowers stand for admiration, making them perfect for expressing respect and love.
4. **Orchids**: Representing luxury and exotic beauty, orchids serve as a fabulous choice for someone who appreciates unique and sophisticated gifts.

By understanding the language of flowers, customers can select arrangements that resonate deeply with the recipient, enhancing the emotional impact of their gift.
