## FLOWER VARIETIES AND THEIR MEANINGS

Flowers convey emotions that words often cannot express. Each variety carries a unique meaning, adding depth to any occasion:

1. **Roses** symbolize love and passion. Red roses are particularly popular for romantic gestures, while pink roses signify gratitude and admiration.
  
2. **Lilies** represent purity and refined beauty. They are commonly chosen for celebrations or memorials, signifying a profound connection.

3. **Sunflowers** radiate happiness and positivity. Their vibrant yellow hue makes them an ideal gift for someone needing a pick-me-up.

4. **Tulips** embody elegance and grace, often associated with spring and renewal, making them a perfect choice for birthdays or anniversaries. 

Understanding the meanings behind these flowers enhances the gifting experience, allowing the sender to choose arrangements that resonate with the recipient's sentiments.
