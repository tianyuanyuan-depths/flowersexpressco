## FLOWER VARIETIES AND THEIR MEANINGS

Different flowers symbolize various emotions and messages. Understanding these meanings can enhance the thoughtfulness behind your floral gift.

1. **Roses**: Often associated with love and passion, roses are a classic choice for romantic gestures. Different colors represent different sentiments; for instance, red roses signify deep love, while yellow roses convey friendship.
   
2. **Lilies**: These elegant blooms symbolize purity and refined beauty. They make an excellent choice for occasions like weddings or to celebrate the arrival of a newborn, conveying heartfelt congratulations.

3. **Tulips**: Known for their vibrant colors, tulips represent grace and elegance. They are ideal for celebrating a friend’s achievement or simply brightening someone's day.

4. **Orchids**: Orchids are exotic flowers symbolizing luxury and strength. Gifting orchids can represent admiration and long-lasting friendship, making them suitable for special occasions.

5. **Gerberas**: With their cheerful appearance, gerberas symbolize joy and cheerfulness. They are perfect for uplifting someone's spirits or sending warm wishes.

Choosing the right flower based on these meanings can make your gift even more special. By selecting a bouquet that reflects the recipient's personality or the emotion you want to convey, you truly personalize the gesture.
