## FLOWER VARIETIES AND THEIR MEANINGS

Different types of flowers convey various emotions and messages. **Roses, for instance, symbolize love and passion**; they are popular for romantic gestures. **Lilies represent purity and devotion**, making them suitable for weddings and anniversaries. **Sunflowers embody warmth and happiness**, often chosen for cheerful occasions or to brighten someone’s day. Understanding these meanings helps consumers select the perfect arrangement to match their sentiments.

Moreover, particular flowers are chosen for specific occasions. **Chrysanthemums are often associated with friendship** and are frequently gifted to friends. On the other hand, **orchids signify luxury and elegance**, ideal for celebratory events. By incorporating these flower meanings into your selection process, you can create a more personalized experience for both the sender and recipient.
