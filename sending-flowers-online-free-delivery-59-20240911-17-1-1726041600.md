## VARIETIES AND MEANINGS OF FLOWERS

Flower varieties carry distinct meanings and emotions, making them perfect for different occasions. 

### ROSES
One of the most popular choices, **roses symbolize love and affection**. Different colors convey various sentiments—red is for passionate love, while yellow represents friendship and joy. A bouquet of mixed roses can express a multitude of feelings, making them an ideal choice for romantic partners, friends, or family.

### LILIES
**Lilies signify purity and refined beauty**. They are often chosen for weddings and celebrations as they embody elegance and sophistication. Their strong fragrance and luxurious appearance make them a favorite among many.

### TULIPS
**Tulips represent perfect love and comfort**. Coming in diverse colors, they bring cheerfulness and warmth into any space. Yellow tulips, for example, convey cheerfulness, while purple symbolizes royalty and elegance.
