## SYMBOLISM OF VARIOUS FLOWER VARIETIES

Understanding what different flowers symbolize can enhance the experience of giving and receiving. Each variety carries its meaning and evokes specific emotions. For instance:

2. **Lilies** symbolize purity and refined beauty, often used in funeral arrangements but also popular for joyful occasions like weddings and anniversaries.
