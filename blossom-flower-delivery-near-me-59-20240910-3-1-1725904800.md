### THE MEANINGS BEHIND DIFFERENT FLOWERS

Flowers convey emotions that words often cannot express. Each bloom carries its unique meaning, enriching the act of giving. Here are a few popular options:

1. **Roses:** Universally known as symbols of love, roses come in varying colors, each with different implications. Red roses denote passionate love, while yellow represents friendship and joy.
   
2. **Tulips:** Often associated with spring’s arrival, tulips symbolize rebirth and new beginnings. They make an ideal gift during festive occasions or to celebrate new journeys.

3. **Lilies:** As symbols of purity and refined beauty, lilies are perfect for formal events such as weddings or to express sympathy during challenging times.

4. **Chrysanthemums:** These blooms represent optimism and joy, making them a great choice for uplifting someone’s spirits.

Understanding these meanings can aid customers in selecting the most appropriate flowers for their specific context, enhancing the thoughtfulness behind their gift.
