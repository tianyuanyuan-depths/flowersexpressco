## FLOWER VARIETIES AND THEIR MEANINGS

Understanding the meanings behind various flower types can enhance the message you wish to convey through your floral gift. For instance:

1. **Roses** symbolize love and passion. The color red particularly represents deep emotional love, while yellow roses signify friendship and joy.
2. **Lilies** convey purity and refined beauty. They are often used in arrangements for weddings and celebrations.
3. **Tulips** represent perfect love. Their simplicity and elegance make them a popular choice for numerous occasions.
4. **Orchids** exude luxury and beauty, making them an ideal gift for someone special or for congratulatory purposes.

By selecting flowers that hold significant meanings, you can convey deeper sentiments, making your floral gift even more special.
