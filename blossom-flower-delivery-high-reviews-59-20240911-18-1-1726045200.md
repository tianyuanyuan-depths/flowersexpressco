## FLOWER VARIETIES AND THEIR MEANINGS

Each type of flower carries its unique symbolism and meaning, making them special choices for various occasions. For instance, **roses** are often associated with love and passion, making them a preferred choice for romantic gestures. Conversely, **lilies** symbolize purity and refined beauty, ideal for celebrations like graduations or housewarmings. Moreover, **sunflowers** exude happiness and positivity, perfect for bringing joy to someone’s day.

### FLOWER CHOICES FOR SPECIAL OCCASIONS

When selecting flowers for a specific occasion, understanding the meanings behind them can help express sentiments more effectively. For birthdays, vibrant and cheerful blooms like tulips or daisies are often favored, while for sympathy, softer shades of roses or white lilies convey compassion and serenity. Choosing the right flowers can make your gesture much more meaningful and personal.
