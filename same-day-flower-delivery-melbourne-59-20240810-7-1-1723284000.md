## MEANINGS BEHIND VARIOUS FLOWER TYPES

Flowers carry significant meanings, with each variety symbolizing different sentiments and emotions. Understanding these meanings can enhance the experience of sending flowers. For example:

1. **Roses**: Commonly known as symbols of love, each color of rose communicates a different message—red for passion, white for purity, and yellow for friendship.
   
2. **Lilies**: These elegant flowers often represent beauty and purity. They are great for expressing heartfelt sentiments during occasions such as weddings or memorials.

3. **Sunflowers**: A bright and cheerful option, sunflowers symbolize adoration and loyalty. They can bring positivity and warmth to any setting.

4. **Daisies**: Associated with innocence and purity, daisies are perfect for conveying simple affection and joy. They are often used in casual settings or to celebrate new beginnings.

By selecting flowers that align with the intended message, customers can create a more meaningful impact with their gifts, enhancing the overall experience of giving flowers.
