## UNDERSTANDING FLOWER VARIETIES AND THEIR MEANINGS

When sending flowers, it's important to consider the meanings behind different varieties. Each flower possesses its unique symbolism and emotional resonance, allowing senders to convey specific sentiments. 

1. **Roses:** Often a symbol of love, particularly romantic love; red roses signify passion, while white roses convey innocence and purity. 
2. **Lilies:** These elegant blooms represent purity and refined beauty, making them ideal for celebrations such as weddings or as sympathy gifts.
3. **Chrysanthemums:** Associated with friendship, joy, and optimism, these cheerful flowers are perfect for congratulatory occasions or to express appreciation.

By selecting the right flowers, senders can enhance their message and sentiment, making the act of giving flowers even more meaningful.
