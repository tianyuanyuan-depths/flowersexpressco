## VARIETIES OF FLOWERS AND THEIR MEANINGS

Understanding flower symbolism can elevate the significance of your gift. Different flowers convey different emotions and are suitable for various occasions. For instance:

1. **Roses** represent love and passion, often chosen for romantic events. They are the go-to for Valentine's Day or anniversaries.
   
2. **Lilies** symbolize purity and refined beauty, making them ideal for celebrating new beginnings, such as births or weddings.

3. **Sunflowers** evoke warmth and happiness, often used to brighten someone's day or express friendship.

4. **Orchids** convey luxury and strength, perfect for sophisticated gifts.

5. **Chrysanthemums** are seen as symbols of optimism and joy, commonly sent during celebrations or cheerful occasions.
