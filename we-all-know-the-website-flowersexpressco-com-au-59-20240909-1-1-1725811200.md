## FLOWER VARIETIES AND THEIR MEANINGS

Different flowers convey various meanings, making the choice of blooms an essential part of sending flowers. Here are several popular varieties and their symbolic significances:

1. **Roses**: Often associated with love and passion, roses are perfect for romantic gestures. The colour of the rose also plays a crucial role—red for love, yellow for friendship, and white for purity.
   
2. **Lilies**: These elegant blooms symbolize devotion and purity. They are often given during celebrations or to express sympathy.

3. **Tulips**: Known for their vibrant colors, tulips represent cheerfulness and are a great choice for uplifting someone's spirits.

4. **Daisies**: Representing innocence and purity, daisies are often included in arrangements meant to convey childhood memories or simplicity.

Through understanding these meanings, customers can select blooms that resonate with their sentiments, enhancing the emotional impact of their gift.
